# Homepage redesign direction

## Chosen direction
**Human systems, clearly organised** — retain Modcon HR's modernist grid, warm paper ground, near-black ink, Archivo typography, and signal-red accent, while bringing more warmth and movement into the editorial photography and the proof section.

## Design dimensions
- **Design movement:** Editorial operations magazine meets precise HR infrastructure.
- **Core principles:** Keep the grid, sharpen hierarchy, add human energy through image treatment, and replace abstract number-led proof with recognizable capability marks.
- **Color philosophy:** Ink and paper remain dominant. Signal red is reserved for focus, motion, and small moments of emphasis. Photography is warm-neutral rather than flat grayscale.
- **Layout paradigm:** Strong rules and rectangular frames, with layered image plates and a compact horizontal capability strip below the hero.
- **Signature elements:** Duotone editorial imagery, a red corner flag, subtle grid lines, and repeated Modcon mark tiles paired with capability labels.
- **Interaction philosophy:** Calm, direct, low-friction; hover states should feel like a physical editorial plate shifting into focus.
- **Animation:** Minimal image scale on hover and gentle mark reveal; no decorative motion that competes with the CTA.
- **Typography system:** Existing Archivo display and mono utility labels remain unchanged.
- **Brand essence:** Honest, exact, human-first operations software.
- **Brand voice:** Direct, credible, practical, warm without becoming casual.
- **Wordmark/logo:** Reuse the existing Modcon HR mark and lockup; do not invent customer logos.
- **Signature brand color:** Signal Red (#EC3013) against Ink (#201E1D) and Paper (#F3F2F2).

## Scope
Refresh the public landing page only. Reuse the existing curated image set, improve its treatment with CSS, replace hero metric pillars with a logo-led capability strip, and remove numeric plate/case labels from the visual proof moments.
